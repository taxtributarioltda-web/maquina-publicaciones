Purpose of the app

Maquina de publicaciones is a private desktop publishing tool used by TAX Tributario to upload approved short-form videos to social media accounts owned or managed by the business.

TikTok integration

The app may connect to TikTok using TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API. This connection is used only to authenticate the TikTok account authorized by the account owner and to upload approved videos as drafts to that account.
