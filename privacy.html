Purpose of the app

Maquina de publicaciones is a private desktop publishing tool used by TAX Tributario to upload approved short-form videos to social media accounts owned or managed by the business.

Data collected

The app does not collect data from public users. It does not allow third-party users to register, create accounts, or submit content.

TikTok data usage

When connected to TikTok, the app may use basic account information only to confirm the identity of the authorized TikTok account. The app uses video upload permission only to upload approved videos as drafts to the authorized TikTok account.

How data is used

Data obtained from TikTok is used only to authenticate the account and upload approved videos. The app does not sell, rent, share, or analyze TikTok user data for advertising or profiling purposes.

Data storage

The app may store local configuration files on the owner's computer to operate the publishing workflow. It does not maintain a public user database.

Third-party services

The app may interact with third-party platforms such as TikTok or other social media services only for the purpose of publishing approved business content.
